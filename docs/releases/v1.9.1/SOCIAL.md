# Social copy — ZenNotes iPhone 1.9.1

## Short

ZenNotes 1.9.1 removes the dark block that could appear behind Apple's rounded
keyboard. The exposed area now follows your ZenNotes light or dark theme for a
cleaner writing view.

## Launch post

ZenNotes 1.9.1 for iPhone and iPad fixes the remaining keyboard backdrop issue
reported after 1.9.

On newer iOS versions, the rounded system keyboard can reveal part of the native
app window when ZenNotes resizes for typing. That exposed area previously
appeared black. It now matches the active ZenNotes theme, including when you
switch between light and dark modes.

The keyboard is still Apple's system keyboard. There are no new permissions,
services, or data collection changes — just a cleaner transition from your note
to the keyboard.

## Note

Thanks to the reporter who followed up after 1.9 and helped us identify that the
first presentation adjustment did not cover every newer iOS version.
