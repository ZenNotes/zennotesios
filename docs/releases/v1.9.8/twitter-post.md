ZenNotes for iPhone and iPad 1.9.8 is out.

Widgets. New Note on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, one tap to a fresh note. Recent Notes, pinned first. Today's Tasks, overdue first, a tap away from the task's line. They wear your theme and update within seconds of a change.

App core 2.46 underneath: comment threads with names, a calendar in the @ menu, saved Tasks filters, a Kanban board with one column per folder.
